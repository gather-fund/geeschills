## 3. What works on TikTok and Instagram

- **The first 2 seconds decide everything.** Open with movement (ice pouring, lid opening) or a bold line on screen. No logo intros.
- **Text on screen.** Many people watch with the sound off. Keep text in the middle of the frame; the bottom 25% gets covered by buttons and captions.
- **Sound matters.** The crunch and clink of ice is your signature. Record it clean, then add a trending sound low underneath. Business accounts can only use the TikTok Commercial Music Library and Instagram's licensed audio. Afrobeats and amapiano trends in those libraries work well.
- **Faces win.** A team member talking to camera for 10 seconds beats a perfect product shot. English, Twi or Pidgin all work; mix them naturally.
- **Talk like a person, not an ad.** "Your guests deserve cold drinks" beats "Premium beverage chilling solutions".
- **Reply to comments with videos.** When someone asks "how much?", answer with a video (Script 8). It's free content and builds trust.

- **Instagram/Facebook:** "Boost" a Reel with the **WhatsApp messages** goal.
- **Audience:** Accra and Tema, ages 24–50, interests: weddings, event planning, parties, Christmas.
- **Budget to start:** GHS 50–100 a day on 1–2 videos for 7 days, then keep only the one that brings the most WhatsApp chats.
- **No alcohol in paid posts.** Use sobolo, water, Malta and soft drinks.
