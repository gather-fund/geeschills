# GEES CHILLS social media plan

Goal: get people in Accra to send a WhatsApp message asking to book your drinks chilling service. Every post points to **WhatsApp 027 794 6635**.

## 1. Set up (week 1)

| Channel | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp Business** | Where bookings happen | Business profile with logo, hours, address, geeschills.com. Add a catalogue (Small get-together, Big celebration, Everything in between). Set a greeting message, quick replies (prices, what's included, payment), and labels (New enquiry, Quoted, Booked, Paid, Done). |
| **Instagram** | Looks and trust for weddings and birthdays | Same handle everywhere (check `geeschills` first). Bio: "Drinks chilling service for events in Ghana · Ice-cold drinks, all day · WhatsApp 027 794 6635 · Call 020 226 2120". Link to geeschills.com. |
| **TikTok** | Reach new people for free | Post the same short videos as Instagram Reels. |
| **Facebook page** | Older customers, funerals, churches, community groups | Share every Reel there too. Join Accra event, wedding and funeral-planning groups. |
| **Google Business Profile** | Shows up for "drinks chilling service Accra" and "cooler hire Accra" searches (people still search the second) and on Maps | Add photos, phone, website and service area. Ask every happy client for a Google review. |

## 2. What to post

Short vertical videos (7–20 seconds) do best. Film every job on your phone.

- **The ice shot.** Ice pouring over drinks, lid opening, cold bottle pulled out. Hook: "This is what your guests see first."
- **Setup in 15 seconds.** Arriving, setting up, cooler full. Hook: "From empty to ice cold in 20 minutes."
- **At the event.** Coolers at a wedding, birthday or funeral (ask the host first). Hook: "Saturday's wedding in East Legon."
- **Client voice.** A 5-second clip or WhatsApp screenshot from a happy client.
- **Which package?** Carousel: small get-together vs big celebration vs in between.
- **Questions people ask.** "How long does the ice last?", "Do you deliver to Tema?", "Can you supply waiters?"
- **Behind the scenes.** Cleaning coolers, loading the car, early morning ice run.

Keep drinks non-alcoholic in anything you pay to promote (sobolo, water, malt, soft drinks). Ghana FDA rules and Meta's ad policy restrict alcohol ads.

## 3. Weekly rhythm

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Reel/TikTok from the weekend's events |
| Wed | Tip, question, or package carousel |
| Fri | "Booking for this weekend? We still have slots to chill your drinks." Reel + WhatsApp Status |
| Daily | WhatsApp Status: 1–3 photos or clips. Your contacts are your warmest leads. |

About 30 minutes, three times a week, is enough. Batch-film at every event.

## 4. Offline to online

- **Stickers on every cooler.** Each event puts your number in front of 50–300 guests. The QR opens WhatsApp with "I saw your cooler at an event".
- **Fliers and cards.** Leave them with event planners, decorators, caterers, DJs, MCs, venues, churches, funeral homes and canopy/chair rental shops. Offer a referral fee (for example 10% of the booking) for every client they send.
- **After every job,** ask the host for a review, a tag, and if you can post photos.

The QR codes fill in a different first message, so you'll know where each customer came from:

| Source | Message they send |
|---|---|
| Flier | "…I saw your flier…" |
| Business card | "…I got your card…" |
| Cooler sticker | "…I saw your cooler at an event…" |

Also ask every new enquiry "How did you hear about us?" and note it in your orders sheet.

## 5. Paid ads (after you have 6–9 good videos)

- Use **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** on Instagram/Facebook, so the ad opens a chat with you.
- Start with GHS 50–100 a day for 7 days, boosting your best-performing Reel.
- Target Accra/Tema, ages 24–50, interests: weddings, event planning, parties.
- Judge ads by the number of WhatsApp chats and bookings, not likes.
