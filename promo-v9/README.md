# GEES CHILLS promo v9

24 seconds, vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC (−16 LUFS, true peak −2.4 dBFS).

## What changed from v8
- All stock and third-party photos (the Coke-on-ice photo, the Malta cans, the soft-drinks cooler) are replaced with Gees Chills' own event photos in `assets/`. The opening clip is unchanged.
- One tagline from the website runs throughout: **Good times. Cold drinks.** It opens and closes the ad.
- "Cooler hire" is said in the first second. Services (ice, delivery, setup, collection) and event types (weddings, birthdays, funerals, corporate) now appear on screen.
- End card has a real call to action: **WhatsApp 027 794 6635** and **geeschills.com**, with no tappable-looking "DM" button.
- Dark caption panel on every photo shot. All key text sits above y≈1440, so the Reels/TikTok caption and buttons don't cover it.
- Small headings above each headline enlarged from 23px to up to 30px. "GHANA" is now aligned to the right edge of the divider line.
- The middle white layout is a 2×2 grid of your photos that build in one by one; it replaces the static single can.
- Light sharpening on the photos. Thumbnail is now the opening shot instead of the white end card.

## Fonts
On a Mac, `render.py` uses DIN Condensed Bold and Arial as before. This export was rendered on Linux with Nimbus Sans Narrow Bold (bundled in `fonts/`) and Liberation Sans as stand-ins. Re-run `python3 render.py` on your Mac for the exact v8 typography.
